Three programme closures each year, mirroring conventional studio and institutional rhythm.

Specifically:

  • August 2026: full month closure (the first full closure period, three weeks after programme start).
  • Christmas 2026 → New Year 2027: Thu 24 Dec 2026 – Mon 4 Jan 2027.
  • Easter 2027: Mon 29 Mar – Fri 2 Apr 2027 (the week after Easter; Good Friday is 26 Mar).

Closures align with Tate's quieter periods and with conventional UK studio practice. They also give Cass real rest at the points in the year people naturally want it.

Reporting

Each six-month period concludes with a technical report jointly produced by Fleury Studio and Tate — drafted by Simon with contributions and sign-off from Jacqueline Moon, and Cass's own reflection forming part of the record. Four reports across the programme:

  • End of Period 1 — around December 2026
  • End of Period 2 — around June 2027
  • End of Period 3 — around December 2027
  • Final report and competency sign-off — around June 2028

Where a second placement is running, the relevant supervisor contributes a short feedback note at each assessment point. QEST receives the reports as part of the funding record.

Default monthly shape

One steady studio day each week with Simon, plus three weeks of two consecutive external days at Tate (or, where it fits, a second placement). The fourth week is studio-only. This holds Jacqui's preference for consecutive blocks, keeps a continuous thread of supervision with Simon throughout the month, and gives Cass a predictable rhythm.

Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri
Week 1 Studio Tate Tate
Week 2 Studio Tate Tate
Week 3 Studio Tate Tate
Week 4 Studio

The specific weekdays are illustrative. The studio day can move; Tate's two-day block can sit anywhere across the week. Friday is left clear throughout. Where a second placement runs, it substitutes into one of the Tate weeks.

In a five-week month, the extra week absorbs whatever moved (illness, institutional dates, the August buffer either side). In months containing a closure week (December, January, April), one of the four weeks is the closure rather than a working week.
