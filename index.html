Working pattern is roughly ten days a month, with breaks built in (see Closures below). Seven-hour day, so 700 hours a year and 1,400 hours across the programme.

Closures

Three programme closures each year, mirroring conventional studio and institutional rhythm.

  • Christmas 2026 → New Year 2027: Thu 24 Dec 2026 – Mon 4 Jan 2027 inclusive; resumes Tue 5 Jan 2027.
  • Easter 2027: Mon 29 Mar – Fri 2 Apr 2027 (the week after Easter; Good Friday is 26 Mar); resumes Mon 5 Apr.

  • Christmas 2027 → New Year 2028: Fri 24 Dec 2027 – Mon 3 Jan 2028; resumes Tue 4 Jan 2028 (New Year's Day falls on a Saturday, so the substitute bank holiday is Mon 3 Jan).

August 2026 is not listed: it falls entirely before the 1 September start and is no longer a programme closure.

Closures align with Tate's quieter periods and with conventional UK studio practice. They also give Cass real rest at the points in the year people naturally want it.

Reporting

Each six-month period concludes with a technical report jointly produced by Fleury Studio and Tate — drafted by Simon with contributions and sign-off from Jacquie Moon, and Cass's own reflection forming part of the record. Four reports across the programme:

  • End of Period 1 (Sep 2026 – Feb 2027) — February 2027
  • End of Period 2 (Mar 2027 – Aug 2027) — July 2027
  • End of Period 3 (Sep 2027 – Feb 2028) — February 2028
  • Final report and competency sign-off (Mar 2028 – Aug 2028) — July 2028

Periods 2 and 4 close on an August closure, so their reports are drafted and signed off in July, the last working month, rather than during a shutdown. The period boundaries themselves remain the six-month points.

Where a second placement is running, the relevant supervisor contributes a short feedback note at each assessment point. QEST receives the reports as part of the funding record.

Default monthly shape

Three weeks of two consecutive days at Tate on Wednesday and Thursday, with studio days falling on the Tuesday of each Tate week (paired immediately before the Tate block) and on the Monday of each non-Tate week. Where the month yields more than four studio days under this rule, the earliest Tuesday is dropped to hold the cap at four. This keeps Jacquie's preference for consecutive Tate blocks, gives Cass a steady thread of supervision with Simon, and leaves Friday clear across the week.

Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri
Week 1 Tate Tate
Week 2 Studio Tate Tate
Week 3 Studio Tate Tate
Week 4 Studio
Week 5 Studio

Where a second placement runs, it substitutes into one of the Tate weeks (Wednesday and Thursday become the second-placement days).

The week-1 Tuesday is included only when both it and the week-5 Monday land in the same calendar month and dropping it would otherwise push the count past four. In a four-week month, the extra Monday simply isn't there. In months containing a closure week (December, January, April), one of the working weeks is replaced by the closure.
